How we grow your store, step by step

No guesswork and no black box. Here's exactly what happens from our first call to your growing sales.

  1. 01

    Audit and research

    We study your store, your current ads, your audience and your competitors to find the biggest opportunities.

    Usually the first week

    • Store speed, design and checkout review
    • Ad account and tracking check
    • Competitor ads and offers research
    • A clear action plan you can keep
  2. 02

    Store and creative building

    We build or improve your Shopify store and create ads made to stop the scroll.

    One to four weeks, depending on scope

    • Shopify design and product page upgrades
    • Hooks, scripts and ad angles
    • AI UGC videos and static ad creatives
    • Your approval before anything goes live
  3. 03

    Launch and testing

    We launch your campaigns, test different creatives and audiences, and watch the first results closely.

    First two to three weeks of ads

    • Campaign setup on Facebook and Instagram
    • Several creatives tested side by side
    • Daily checks on spend and results
    • Early report on what's working
  4. 04

    Optimization and scaling

    We put more budget behind the winners, cut the losers and keep feeding the account with fresh creatives.

    Ongoing, every week

    • Budget moved to the best ads and audiences
    • New creatives to beat ad fatigue
    • Store tweaks based on real buyer data
    • Regular reports and strategy calls
